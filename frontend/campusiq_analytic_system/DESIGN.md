## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a premium academic analytics posture: disciplined, intellectually authoritative, and tactilely refined. Built for provosts, deans, researchers, and university administrative leadership, it balances deep institutional trust with modern computational precision.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate Cleanliness** with **Soft Neumorphic Depth**. Rather than heavy 2020-era skeuomorphic extrusions, the interface adopts a restrained, contemporary tactile language: surfaces feel sculpted from matte physical materials, employing whisper-soft dual shadows, micro-radii bevels, and crisp hairline edges. The emotional response must evoke clarity under data pressure, academic prestige, and effortless legibility across complex multi-tiered metric hierarchies.

## Colors
The system defaults to a focused, fatigue-reducing dark canvas designed for expansive data analysis, while maintaining absolute parity in a high-clarity light mode.

### Dark Mode (Default)
- **Base Canvas:** Deep Charcoal (`#0f172a`).
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Slate Layer 1 (`#1e293b`), Slate Layer 2 (`#334155`).
- **Ambient Accent Glows:** Soft Electric Sky (`#38bdf8`) and Celestial Indigo (`#818cf8`) at 8% to 15% opacity for active states and critical KPI boundaries.
- **Typography:** Primary text (`#f8fafc`), Secondary/Muted text (`#94a3b8`).

### Light Mode
- **Base Canvas:** Crisp slate-tinted warm white (`#f8fafc` to `#f1f5f9`).
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Pure white (`#ffffff`).
- **Typography:** Deep Slate Ink (`#0f172a`), Subordinate text (`#334155`, `#64748b`).

### Academic Semantic Accents
Data categorization and institutional KPIs rely on a strictly regulated categorical spectrum:
- **Academic Governance & Core Metrics (Indigo):** `#4f46e5`
- **Admissions, Enrollment & Fluidity (Cyan):** `#0284c7`
- **Research Grants, Retention & Growth (Emerald):** `#059669`
- **Alerts, At-Risk Forecasting & Action Items (Amber):** `#d97706`
- **Endowment, Graduate Programs & Honors (Violet):** `#7c3aed`

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the balanced, structural authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display, headings, and quantitative KPI callouts with the neutral, hyper-legible ergonomics of **Inter** for narrative body text, dense data tables, and interactive labels.

- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric figures in charts, data tables, and KPI metric ribbons must employ OpenType `tnum` (tabular lining figures) to maintain alignment down columns.
- **Rhythm & Leading:** Dense metric displays leverage snug line-heights, while analytical body copy is spaced generously to mitigate administrative cognitive load.
- **Capitalization:** Use `label-sm` strictly in uppercase with positive letter-spacing (`+0.03em`) for section meta-tags, analytical categories, and status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates on an immutable 8px base unit with a flexible 12-column analytical grid. 

- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer canvas margins. Dashboards default to a persistent 280px left navigation rail with an adaptive main analytical canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px outer margins. Navigation collapses to an icon dock (72px) or off-canvas drawer. Metric summary grids collapse from 4 columns to 2x2.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Complex multi-series charts pivot to horizontally scrollable viewports or stack into modular single-metric cards.
- **Analytical Density Rules:** Grid gaps inside card clusters remain locked to `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) to preserve visual grouping, while intra-component padding inside analytical cards scales from `space-md` to `space-xl` based on data density settings.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through restrained soft neumorphic layering rather than stark dropped shadows or completely flat planar sheets.

### Soft Neumorphic Formula
- **Dark Mode Elevation:**
  - *Base Surface:* `#0f172a`
  - *Resting Cards (Level 1):* Solid `#1e293b` fill, edged with a 1px boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` on top/left, `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)` on bottom/right). Base shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - *Hover / Selected (Level 2):* Upward ambient bloom via soft colored backdrops (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.08)` or `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`) paired with `0 14px 30px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
  - *Sunken / Inset Well (Level 0 - Inputs/Segmented Controls):* Inset shadow `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
- **Light Mode Elevation:**
  - *Resting Cards:* Pure `#ffffff` surface, with dual shadow: `-4px -4px 10px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9), 4px 6px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  - *Sunken Controls:* Inset dual shadow: `inset 2px 2px 5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), inset -2px -2px 5px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.

## Shapes
The visual rhythm balances architectural solidity with approachable curvature. All core containers, data cards, and analytical modules adopt modern large-radius profiles (`rounded-2xl` / 16px), while interactive controls step down in scale for ergonomic precision:
- **Primary Data & Dashboard Cards:** `1rem` (16px, `rounded-2xl`).
- **Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns & Menus:** `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`).
- **Pills, Status Badges & Quick Filters:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / 9999px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Indigo (`#4f46e5`) background, subtle top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), pristine white text. Hover triggers an elevation bloom: `0 4px 14px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Soft Neumorphic:** Slate surface (`#1e293b` dark, `#ffffff` light) matching the card elevation, 1px subtle boundary, muted slate text transitioning to high-contrast white/slate on hover.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background with no border; visual feedback relies strictly on surface color shift to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` (dark) or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` (light).

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- **Structure:** Encased in 16px border radii with 1px hairline perimeter borders. Padding is fixed at `24px` (`space-lg`).
- **Header Pattern:** Left-aligned title with right-aligned progressive disclosure controls (contextual date-range pickers, segmented tab switches, and export actions).
- **Subtle Wells:** Inner chart containers and secondary metrics rest within sunken wells (`inset` shadows) to separate metadata from analytical canvas space.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `11px` uppercase tracking. Background tints utilize academic semantic colors at 12% opacity with solid 100% text color (e.g., Emerald `#059669` background fill at `0.12` alpha with `#10b981` typography).
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Soft neumorphic toggle state; unselected chips sit flush with the canvas, while active filter chips press outward with primary indigo tinting and an active check dot.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- **Fields:** Sunk into the canvas using soft inset shadow geometry, 8px radii, 1px border (`#334155` dark, `#cbd5e1` light). 
- **States:** Focus replaces borders with an electric sky or indigo highlight (`#38bdf8` / `#4f46e5`) backed by a non-blurry 3px outer ring at 15% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** Checkboxes use 6px rounded squares; radio buttons are circular.
- **States:** Unchecked states are subtly debossed. Checked states fill with primary indigo, revealing a crisp white vector icon, backed by a micro glow.

### Segmented Controls & Tabs
- **Rail Style:** Contained within an inset, debossed pill/track (`#0f172a` in dark mode).
- **Active Thumb:** A floating, tactile elevated slate pill (`#1e293b` in dark mode, `#ffffff` in light mode) with a soft drop shadow (`0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`), animating laterally between positions.

### Data Tables
- **Header:** Sticky, muted uppercase typography (`label-sm`), divider-less background, demarcated purely by subtle bottom 1px borders.
- **Row Architecture:** Alternating hover states with 4px rounded row bounds. Row selections highlight with a left-edge 3px indigo indicator bar and an ambient background tint.
